LOGO 3D -- THANK YOU
====================

Thank you to Noel Evans and Dan Mellinger.

All of my inspiration comes from years of playing with their iPad app,
Logo_3D!, and from the list of commands in the readme of Noel's project:
https://github.com/noelevans555/logo3d

This browser version was written from scratch to run the Logo scripts I
made on that app. It does not contain any of their code. The command
names follow theirs, with gratitude.

Thanks also to ChatGPT, which wrote the first versions, and especially
to Claude AI, which made the major improvements. They did most of this.
I was like a backseat driver nagging the limo driver.
