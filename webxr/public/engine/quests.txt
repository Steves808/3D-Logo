LOGO 3D QUESTS
Each quest starts with a line of slashes, then the title.
GOAL: what to do.  HINT: shown when asked.  START: code put in the editor.
SOLUTION: draws the dashed target shape (the answer is checked by shape, not by text).
/////////////////////////
First steps
GOAL: The turtle draws a line when it moves. Make it go forward 100 steps.
HINT: fd means forward. Try: fd 100
START:
fd 50
SOLUTION:
fd 100
/////////////////////////
Turn a corner
GOAL: Draw an L shape: forward 100, turn right 90 degrees, forward 60.
HINT: rt 90 turns right by 90 degrees. Then use fd again.
START:
fd 100
SOLUTION:
fd 100 rt 90 fd 60
/////////////////////////
Square
GOAL: Draw a square with sides of 80. A square is "forward, turn right" four times.
HINT: fd 80 rt 90 fd 80 rt 90 ... four times in all.
START:
fd 80 rt 90
fd 80 rt 90
SOLUTION:
fd 80 rt 90 fd 80 rt 90 fd 80 rt 90 fd 80 rt 90
/////////////////////////
Let the computer repeat
GOAL: Typing the same thing again and again is boring. Draw a square with sides of 120 using repeat.
HINT: repeat 4 [fd 120 rt 90]
START:
repeat 2 [fd 120 rt 90]
SOLUTION:
repeat 4 [fd 120 rt 90]
/////////////////////////
Triangle
GOAL: Draw a triangle with sides of 100. How far must the turtle turn at each corner? (All the turns together make one full spin: 360.)
HINT: 360 divided by 3 corners is 120. repeat 3 [fd 100 rt 120]
START:
repeat 3 [fd 100 rt 90]
SOLUTION:
repeat 3 [fd 100 rt 120]
/////////////////////////
Hexagon
GOAL: Six sides of 60. Use the same trick: the turn is 360 divided by the number of sides. You can let Logo do the division.
HINT: repeat 6 [fd 60 rt 360/6]
START:
repeat 6 [fd 60 rt 90]
SOLUTION:
repeat 6 [fd 60 rt 360/6]
/////////////////////////
Star
GOAL: A five-point star with lines of 150. The turtle turns MORE than a quarter turn at each point.
HINT: Turn 144 at each point: repeat 5 [fd 150 rt 144]
START:
repeat 5 [fd 150 rt 100]
SOLUTION:
repeat 5 [fd 150 rt 144]
/////////////////////////
Lift off! (3D)
GOAL: Now leave the floor. Draw a square that stands up like a window: tilt the turtle up 90 degrees first. Drag the picture to look around it.
HINT: up 90 tilts the nose up. Then draw the square as before: up 90 repeat 4 [fd 80 rt 90]
START:
repeat 4 [fd 80 rt 90]
SOLUTION:
up 90 repeat 4 [fd 80 rt 90]
/////////////////////////
Teach the turtle a new word
GOAL: "to" teaches the turtle a new word. The word sq is already made. Use it to draw a square on the floor AND a square standing up.
HINT: Type sq, then up 90, then sq again.
START:
to sq repeat 4 [fd 80 rt 90] end
sq
SOLUTION:
to sq repeat 4 [fd 80 rt 90] end
sq up 90 sq
/////////////////////////
Staircase
GOAL: Build 5 stairs. One stair is: tilt up 90, forward 20, tilt down 90, forward 20.
HINT: repeat 5 [up 90 fd 20 dn 90 fd 20]
START:
up 90 fd 20 dn 90 fd 20
SOLUTION:
repeat 5 [up 90 fd 20 dn 90 fd 20]
/////////////////////////
Growing spiral
GOAL: A variable is a box that holds a number. Here the box is called s. Make each line 10 longer than the one before, 12 lines in all.
HINT: Inside the repeat, add: make s=s+10
START:
make s=10
repeat 12 [fd s rt 90]
SOLUTION:
make s=10
repeat 12 [fd s rt 90 make s=s+10]
/////////////////////////
Spring
GOAL: Go forward a little, turn a little, tilt up a little — again and again — and you get a spring. Make it 120 steps long.
HINT: repeat 120 [fd 5 rt 10 up 1]
START:
repeat 30 [fd 5 rt 10 up 1]
SOLUTION:
repeat 120 [fd 5 rt 10 up 1]
/////////////////////////
Flower by symmetry
GOAL: symmetry 8 copies everything the turtle draws 8 times around the middle. Draw ONE petal (a triangle with sides of 70) and get a whole flower.
HINT: symmetry 8 repeat 3 [fd 70 rt 120]
START:
symmetry 8
fd 70
SOLUTION:
symmetry 8
repeat 3 [fd 70 rt 120]
/////////////////////////
A tree that draws itself
GOAL: A word can use itself! tree draws a branch, then two smaller trees. It stops when the branch is too small. Grow the tree from size 60.
HINT: The last line should be: tree 60
START:
to tree :s
if :s < 10 [stop]
fd :s
lt 30 tree :s*0.7
rt 60 tree :s*0.7
lt 30 bk :s
end
dn 90
tree 20
SOLUTION:
to tree :s
if :s < 10 [stop]
fd :s
lt 30 tree :s*0.7
rt 60 tree :s*0.7
lt 30 bk :s
end
dn 90
tree 60
